# Attribution

FRAP's subject and starting construction are inspired by Giovanni Luca
Marchetti, *Rubik's Abstract Polytopes* (2025),
[arXiv:2502.13518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13518). The incidence-pair
construction and facet-generated Rubik group are attributed to that work;
they are not FRAP inventions.

Egon Schulte's *Regular Incidence Complexes, Polytopes, and C-Groups*
(2017), [arXiv:1711.02297](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.02297), supplies
background on incidence structures, flags, and regularity. The canonical
book reference is Peter McMullen and Egon Schulte, *Abstract Regular
Polytopes* (Cambridge University Press, 2002).

The [reference inventory](docs/sources.md) identifies projects to compare
before choosing a Lean contribution. Inclusion is not a claim that FRAP
incorporates their code, design, proof, or verified results. This skeleton
contains no implementation from those projects.

Add a specific attribution whenever later work is influenced by another
source, identifying the mathematical or behavioral contribution rather than
giving an undifferentiated list of acknowledgements. Third-party works retain
their own licenses and attribution requirements.
