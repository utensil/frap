# FRAP contributor instructions

- Read `README.md`, `docs/scope.md`, `docs/conventions.md`, and
  `CONTRIBUTING.md` before making changes.
- Ground notes in literature and Lean statements in the notes. Keep source
  claims, informal derivations, and kernel-checked results distinct.
- Do not add substantive Lean until the contribution assessment identifies
  a distinct contribution beyond the designated references.
- Use cleanroom methodology: work from mathematical principles, literature,
  and behavior/specification records. Do not copy or port reference code,
  core designs, or proof architecture. Preserve accurate attribution.
- Record unresolved source or convention questions; do not hide them in
  assumptions or placeholder proofs.
- Keep changes scoped and reviewable. Validate what the change actually
  affects and report material limits of the evidence.
- The initial skeleton PR is human-gated. Do not merge it or enable automatic
  merging without explicit maintainer approval.
- Keep private coordination records, credentials, local paths, and reference
  implementation excerpts out of public artifacts.
